Using NHANES from 2011-2014  we will run a weighted linear regression analysis where the predictor is  HbA1c (%) and outcome is digit symbol score. Covariates include age categorized as <70 or ≥70 years, sex, and race/ethnicity categorized as non-hispanic white,  non-hispanic black, non-hispanic asian, mexican-american, and others. We will include adults age >=60 years and completed cognitive function tests which require digit symbol substitution test (DSST), animal fluency score, and CERAD Score Trial 1 recall, CERAD Score Trial 2 recall, CERAD Score Trial 3 recall, and delayed word recall (CERAD W-L)  tasks and had information on diabetes status that require diabetes self-report of a diagnosis by a physician or health care professional, glycohemoglobin (%) HbA1C, fasting plasma glucose (FPG) (mg/dL).
