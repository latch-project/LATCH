Using NHANES from 2011 to 2020 we will run a logistic regression analysis where the predictor is serum albumin (g/dL) categorized into quartiles (%) Q1: <25, Q2: ≥25 and <50, Q3: ≥50 and <75, Q4: ≥75,  outcome is diabetic retinopathy, covariates include age, gender, and race categorized as non-hispanic white,  non-hispanic black, mexican american, and other race. We will exclude age less than 30 years, age of diabetes onset less than 30 years, missing data for serum albumin, missing data for diabetic retinopathy status. We will exclude who have both borderline self report diabetes status and glycohemoglobin missing or less than 6.5%.
