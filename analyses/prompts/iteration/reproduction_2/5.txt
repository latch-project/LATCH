Using NHANES from 2009 to 2018  we will run a weighted logistic regression using exam weights  where the predictor is lactate dehydrogenase (U/L) categorized  in quartiles(%) Q1: ≤25, Q2: >25 and ≤50, Q3: >50 and ≤75, Q4: >75 and outcome is diabetic retinopathy. Covariates include age, sex, and race, categorized as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, other hispanic, other race. We will exclude participants who had missing values or said “No” or “Borderline”  to the question doctor told you have diabetes, participants who were not told by doctors whether they had diabetic retinopathy, participants with missing lactate dehydrogenase (U/L).
