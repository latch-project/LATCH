Using NHANES from 2005 to 2008 we will run group comparison using exam weights where we are comparing different variables including age, systolic blood pressure (mmHg), diastolic blood pressure (mmHg), glycohemoglobin (%) between groups who responded “Yes” or “No” for any retinopathy worse eye. We will include individuals aged 40 years and older and has diabetes defined as who said “Yes” to the question doctor told you have diabetes or hemoglobin A1C of 6.5% or greater and exclude individuals with missing values for any retinopathy worse eye.
