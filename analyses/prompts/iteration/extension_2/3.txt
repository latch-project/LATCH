Using AIREADI from 2023 to 2025 we will run logistic regression using the exam survey weights where predictor is UACR-SACR ratio,  calculated as ((urine albumin (ug/ml) / urine creatinine(ug/ml))/ (serum albumin ALB (g/dL)/serum creatinine SCR (mg/dL)) and then categorized in quartiles(%) Q1: <=25, Q2: >25 and <=50, Q3: >50 and <=75, Q4: >75. Outcome is diabetic retinopathy. Covariates include age (years), Excluded participants who did not have type ii diabetes. Participants with missing values in  diabetic retinopathy in one or both eyes were excluded. Participants with missing serum albumin ALB (g/dL) or serum creatinine SCR (mg/dL) or urine albumin (ug/ml) or urine creatinine(ug/ml)  will be excluded. Participants who were less than 20 years old were excluded.
