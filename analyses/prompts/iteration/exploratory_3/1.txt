Using NHANES data from 1999-2008, we will perform a prevalence analysis of diabetes, defined by diagnosis or glycohemoglobin level. Use weighted prevalence if available. We will group by a vision category  'vision group', defined using the better vision eye among right and left eye: "Group 1" No VI (Reference Group): visual acuity, presenting is 20/40 or better. "Group 2" Correctable VI (CVI): visual acuity, presenting is worse than 20/40, but visual acuity with refraction is 20/40 or better. "Group 3" Nonrefractive VI (NVI): visual acuity, presenting  is worse than 20/40, and visual  acuity with refraction  is still worse than 20/40. Include people whose age (years) is equal or more than 20, and exclude participants with missing vision values, age, who are missing both diabetes diagnosis or glycohemoglobin level.
