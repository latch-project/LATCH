Using NHANES from 1999-2010 we will run a Cox regression  where the predictor is red cell distribution categorized in quartiles (Q1: <= 25%, Q2: <25 and <=50%, Q3: <50 and <=75%, Q4: >75%), outcome is all-cause mortality, time-to-event is follow up duration. Covariates include age, gender, race, and hemoglobin. We will include participants age equal or more than 18, self-reported diabetes, and exclude participants missing red cell distribution or all-cause mortality data.
