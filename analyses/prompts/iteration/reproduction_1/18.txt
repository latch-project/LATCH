Using NHANES from 1999 to 2018  we will run a weighted Cox regression using exam weights where the predictor is uric acid levels (mg/dl) categorized as quintiles as cut-offs QN1: <20%, QN2: >=20 and <40%, QN3: >=40 and <60%, QN4: >=60 and <80%, QN5: >=80%. Outcome is all-cause mortality, time-to-event is follow up duration. Covariates include age, sex, race categorized  as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, mexican american or hispanic, and other. We will include participants with diabetes at baseline were defined as having been diagnosed by physicians or as having a glycated hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) value 6.5% or a fasting plasma glucose level 126 mg/dL. The patients with diabetes who had at least one of the following conditions were excluded: 1) individuals aged less than 20 years; 2) a pregnancy at baseline; 3) having cancer at baseline; 4) missing information on serum uric acid levels, estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) which requires serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, and sex, race,  and HbA1c values; and 5) for whom information on death status was unavailable.
