Using NHANES from 2007 to 2018 we will run a weighted logistic regression using exam weights where the predictor is weight adjusted waist ratio which is calculated as waist circumference (cm) divide by the square root of weight(kg), categorized as quartiles(%) Q1: ≤25, Q2: >25 and ≤50, Q3: >50 and ≤75, Q4: >75. The outcome of interest is the presence of kidney disease, defined as either an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below 60 mL/min/1.73m^2 or a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) (mg/g) of 30 mg/g or greater.  eGFR can be calculated using gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, race information for 4 different cases: 1. if gender is female and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 * 1.159 2. if gender is female and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 3. if gender is male and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.159 4. if gender is male and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age).  Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (mg/g) is calculated as 100*(urine albumin mg/L / urine creatinine mg/dL). Covariates include race categorized as white or non white, age categorized as less than 60 and 60 or more, and gender. We will include diabetes patients where diabetes was defined as one of the following: self-reported doctor diagnosis of diabetes, or fasting plasma glucose (FPG, mmol/L) ≥7.0 mmol/L, or glycohemoglobin≥6.5%, or the use of diabetes medication. We will exclude participants under the age of 20, pregnant, as well as those without data on WWI index that requires waist circumference (cm) and weight (kg), urinary albumin to urinary creatinine ratio that requires urine albumin (mg/L) and urine creatinine (mg/dL), missing  eGFR that requires, age, race, gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl).
