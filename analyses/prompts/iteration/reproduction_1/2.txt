Using NHANES from 2005 to 2008  we will run a logistic regression  where the predictor is  monocyte count/lymphocyte count*10 and outcome is proliferative diabetic retinopathy. Covariates include age, sex, race categorized as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, mexican american, and other. We will include  patients whose age is equal or more  than 40 years old, has diabetes defined as as one of the following (1) Glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) ≥ 6.5%,(2) Fasting plasma glucose (FPG) ≥ 7 mmol/L; (3) during an oral glucose tolerance test, 2-h plasma glucose ≥ 11.1 mmol/L; (4) self-report questionnaire data indicating physician diagnosis of diabetes; and (5) lower blood glucose by current use of insulin or diabetes pill. Exclude participants with missing data on retinopathy grading, and missing  peripheral blood MLR calculated as monocyte count/lymphocyte count * 10 and without diabetic retinopathy.
