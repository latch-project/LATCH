Using NHANES from 2009 to 2016 we will run  logistic regression using the exam survey weights where predictor is UACR-SACR ratio,  calculated as urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio UACR (mg/g)/ (serum albumin ALB (g/dL)/serum creatinine SCR (mg/dL))) and then categorized in quartiles(%) Q1: <=25, Q2: >25 and <=50, Q3: >50 and <=75, Q4: >75. Outcome is diabetic retinopathy. Covariates include age (years), sex, race categorized as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, mexican american, other hispanic, and other race, education categorized as primary school, secondary, school, college or above, or not recorded, poverty income ratio (PIR), categorized as Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, and Q_unknown (for missing values). Participants did not know or were not told by doctors whether they had diabetes were excluded. Participants who were on the borderline of diabetes were excluded. Missing or refused to answer the diabetes question will be excluded. Participants who were not told by doctors whether they had diabetic retinopathy were excluded. Participants with missing serum albumin ALB (g/dL) or serum creatinine SCR (mg/dL) or urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio UACR (mg/g) will be excluded. Participants who were less than 20 years old were excluded.
