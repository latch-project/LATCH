Using NHANES from 2001 to 2008  we will run a weighted prevalence using exam weights where the prevalence of ALB-CKD is defined as having both an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below 60 mL/min/1.73m^2 and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) of 30 mg/g or greater. eGFR can be calculated using gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, race information for 4 different cases: 1. if gender is female and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 * 1.159 2. if gender is female and race is  not  Black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 3. if gender is male and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.159 4. if gender is male and race is not black: 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age). Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (mg/g) is calculated as 100*(urine albumin mg/L / urine creatinine mg/dL) Include participants whose age is older than 20 years, include participants who meet any of the diabetes criteria: self-reported diabetes diagnosis is yes, glycohemoglobin (HbA1C) >= 6.5%, or fasting glucose (mg/dl)>=126. Exclude participants with missing sociodemographic information including race, age, gender, and missing data for eGFR which requires serum creatinine (mg/dl)  and urine albumin to creatinine ratio (UACR).
