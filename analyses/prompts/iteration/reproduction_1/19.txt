Using NHANES from 2005 to 2018 we will run a weighted Cox regression where the predictor is stress hyperglycemia ratio which is defined as fasting plasma glucose (mmol/l)/ (1.59* glycohemoglobin (%)-2.59), categorized as quartiles, categorized in quartiles(%) Q1: <=25, Q2: >25 and <=50, Q3: >50 and <=75, Q4: >75, outcome is all-cause mortality, time-to-event is follow up duration. Covariates include age, sex, race. We will exclude participants with missing data on admission glucose, HbA1c, mortality data, age less than 20 years old and without diabetes or prediabetes when diabetes defined as meeting any of these: glycohemoglobin (%)>=6.5 or fasting plasma glucose >=126 mg/dl, doctor told you have diabetes yes or taking insulin now yes. Prediabetes is defined as meeting any of these: glycohemoglobin (%) >=5.7 less or equal to 6.4 fasting plasma glucose level between 100-125 mg/dl or yes to do you have prediabetes.
