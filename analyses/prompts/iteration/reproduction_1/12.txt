Using NHANES  from 2011 to 2020 we will run a logistic regression where the predictor is red blood cell folate (nmol/l), categorized in quintiles(%) QN1: ≤20, QN2: >20 and ≤40, QN3: >40 and ≤60, QN4: >60 and ≤80, QN5: >80. Outcome is the presence of kidney disease, defined as either an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below 60 mL/min/1.73m^2 or a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) (mg/g) of 30 mg/g or greater.  eGFR can be calculated using gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, race for 4 different cases:  1. if gender is female and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 * 1.159. 2. if gender is female and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018. 3. if gender is male and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.159. 4. if gender is male and race is not Black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age). Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (mg/g) is calculated as 100*(urine albumin mg/L / urine creatinine mg/dL). Covariates include age, sex, and race. We will include participants with  diabetes defined as  defined as meeting one of the criteria: having self-reported diabetes, fasting plasma glucose >= 7.0 mmol/L, glycohemoglobin (HbA1c) (%) >=6.5 , or random glucose level >= 11.1 mmol/L. Exclude participants whose age is less than 18 years, who has missing folate data that requires total folate (nmol/L) and red blood cell folate (nmol/l), missing diabetic kidney disease data that requires gender, race, age, serum creatinine (mg/dl), urine albumin mg/L, urine creatinine mg/dL.
