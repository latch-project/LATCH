Using NHANES from 2005 to 2008 we will run group comparison using exam weights where we are comparing different variables including age, systolic blood pressure (mmHg), diastolic blood pressure (mmHg), glycohemoglobin (%) between groups with and without diabetic retinopathy. We will include individuals aged 40 years and older and has diabetes defined as self-report of previous diagnosis of the disease by a clinician or hemoglobin A1C of 6.5% or greater and exclude individuals with missing diabetic retinopathy grading.
