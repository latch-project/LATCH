Using NHANES from 2005 to 2008 we will run weighted logistic regression using exam weights where the predictor is hdl-cholesterol, categorized in  tertiles(%) T1: ≤33, T2: >33 and ≤66, T3: >66. Outcome is diabetic retinopathy based on  retinopathy grading. Covariates include age, gender, race, categorized as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, mexican american, other race. We will include participants with diabetes defined as one of  self-reported diabetes, use of insulin or anti-diabetes drugs, hemoglobin Alc level ≥ 6.5%, oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) ≥ 11.1mmol/L, or serum glucose (Glu) ≥ 7.0mmol/L. We will exclude non-diabetic patients, missing data of hdl-cholesterol or retinopathy grading.
