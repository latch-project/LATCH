Using NHANES from 1999-2020 we will run a weighted logistic regression using exam weights where the predictor is neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio defined as neutrophil count / lymphocyte count, categorized in tertiles (%) T1: <=33, T2: >33 and <=66, T3: >66. The outcome is  the presence of kidney disease, defined as either an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below 60 mL/min/1.73m^2 or a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) (mg/g) of 30 mg/g or greater.  eGFR can be calculated using gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, race  for 4 different cases: 1. if gender is female and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 * 1.159. 2. if gender is female and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018. 3. if gender is male and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.159. 4. if gender is male and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age). Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (mg/g) is calculated as 100*(urine albumin mg/L / urine creatinine mg/dL). Covariates include age, sex, race, categorized as non-hispanic white, non-hispanic black, mexican american, other hispanic, other races. We will exclude participants age less than 20 years, incomplete data of albumin to creatine ratio (ACR) that requires urine albumin (mg/lL) and urine creatinine (mg/dL), incomplete data of eGFR that requires gender, age, race, serum creatinine (mg/dl), incomplete data of neutrophil to lymphocyte ratio that requires neutrophil count, lymphocyte count, also exclude cancer patients, exclude pregnant patients, exclude participants without diabetes where diabetes is defined as meeting any of the following: yes to having self-reported diabetes, the use of insulin, use of other diabetes drugs, fasting glucose (mmol/l) ≥ 7.0, glycosylated hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) (%) > 6.5.
