Using NHANES  from 2011 to 2018  we will run a weighted logistic regression analysis using exam weights where the predictor is systemic immune-inflammation index calculated by platelet count * neutrophil count / lymphocyte count, categorized as low (<445.21) and high (>=445.21). The outcome is the presence of kidney disease defined as either an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below 60 mL/min/1.73m^2 or a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) (mg/g) of 30 mg/g or greater. eGFR can be calculated using gender, serum creatinine (mg/dl), age, race information for 4 different cases:1. if gender is female and race is black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 * 1.159 2. if gender is female and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -0.329 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.7, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.018 3. if gender is male and race is black: 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age) * 1.159 4. if gender is male and race is not black : 141 * min(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -0.411 * max(serum creatinine (mg/dl) / 0.9, 1) ** -1.209 * (0.993 ** age). We will exclude participants whose age is less than 18 years, incomplete data of SSI that requires platelet count and neutrophil count and lymphocyte count, incomplete data of urinary albumin to creatinine ratio (UACR), and exclude data of eGFR that requires race, age, gender, exclude participants without diabetes if they did not meet any of the following criteria for diabetes: told by medical professionals they had diabetes, fasting plasma glucose (mmol/L) >= 7, glycohemoglobin (HbA1C) >= 6.5mmol/L, taking diabetes medication. Also  exclude pregnant participants.
